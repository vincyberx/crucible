# Task: knights and knaves

On an island every inhabitant is either a **knight**, who always tells the truth, or a
**knave**, who always lies. You meet six inhabitants, A, B, C, D, E and F, and each makes
one statement:

- **A:** "C is a knave, or E is a knight, or both."
- **B:** "Exactly four of the six of us are knights."
- **C:** "At least four of the six of us are knaves."
- **D:** "F and B are of different types."
- **E:** "If C is a knight, then D is a knight."
- **F:** "Exactly one of A, C and E is a knight."

Conventions: "the six of us" means all six inhabitants, the speaker included. A statement
of the form "if P, then Q" is false only when P is true and Q is false.

**Question:** which of the six are knights? (The answer is unique.)

## Deliverable

Write your reasoning to `ANSWER.md` in the current directory. The file must end with a
final line of exactly this form, listing the knights' letters separated by commas
(order does not matter), e.g. `ANSWER: A,C`:

```
ANSWER: <value>
```
